Scope: the soap dispenser only; this condition does not apply to the sponge.

The pinned success predicate requires the soap dispenser to be upright using
`check_obj_upright`: absolute object roll and pitch must each be less than 15
degrees. Inspect its standing orientation after release using fresh public views;
proximity to the sink does not prove posture. This is a posture reference, not a
measurement of hidden orientation or a complete task checklist. The language for
