# WeighIngredients: upright packaged ingredient

Scope: the packaged ingredient being weighed, not the cabinet or scale.

The pinned success predicate requires the packaged ingredient to be upright using
`check_obj_upright`: absolute object roll and pitch must each be less than 15
degrees. Reaching the scale while lying on its side does not satisfy this posture
condition. After release, inspect the package's standing orientation in fresh
public views; retain uncertainty when it is occluded. This is a posture reference,
not a measurement of the current object's hidden orientation or a complete task
checklist.
